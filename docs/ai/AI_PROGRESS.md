## Runtime validation
**Passed on 2026-10-01** against `pgvector/pgvector:pg16` (PostgreSQL 16.15, pgvector 0.8.6), in an isolated compose project with throwaway credentials. It is repeatable: `AiDatabaseSecurityTests` and `AiUsageStoreIntegrationTests` (and, since AI-006, `AiKnowledgeIntegrationTests`; and since AI-007 `AiEmbeddingIntegrationTests`; since AI-008 `AiRetrievalIntegrationTests` and since AI-009 `AiRagIntegrationTests`; 38 tests in all) run whenever `AI_TEST_DB_OWNER` and `AI_TEST_DB_RUNTIME` are set, and are skipped otherwise. Recipe in `AI_REPO_MAP.md`.

Proven: database initialises; pgvector usable; all migrations apply through the service's own bootstrap, twice, and are recorded; `ai_app` logs in only with its password, is not superuser, has no `BYPASSRLS`, owns nothing; row-level security enabled and forced on every school table; no school set means no rows and no inserts; each school reads and writes only its own rows, in both directions; the school of a transaction ends with it; a reused pooled connection carries no school (with the pool's own reset switched off); `ai_app` cannot update or delete usage or audit, write settings, read `schema_migrations`, create objects, change policies, switch role or turn row security off; simultaneous reservations cannot overspend. Re-run on 2026-10-02 with the knowledge tables: same results, plus knowledge documents and chunks invisible without a school and across schools, a chunk cannot be attached to a document of another school, a failed write leaves nothing, and `ai_app` cannot update either table or delete chunks on their own. Re-run again with vector storage (AI-007): real pgvector insert, read-back and distance; a vector of another dimension refused by the application and by the table; vectors invisible without a school and across schools, including through a nearest-neighbour query; a vector cannot be attached to a chunk of another school; re-embedding replaces without duplicating; a document is never `ready` without a vector for every chunk; `ai_app` can change only the embedding outcome columns of a document. Re-run with retrieval (AI-008): nearest neighbours come back in exact cosine order; each school finds only its own chunks even when the query is the exact vector of another school's chunk and even with the school filter removed from the query; no school means no result; documents that are not ready, are in an earlier space or are addressed to another audience are never found. Re-run with the assistant (AI-009): a document of School A goes through chunks, vectors, retrieval and context to the fake model and comes back as an answer with School A's source; School B, asking the same question, gets `insufficient-knowledge` while it has no documents and only its own source afterwards, whatever it sends; usage rows are per school with the number of chunks used and hold no text.

Rerun these tests whenever a migration or `AiDatabase` changes, and before any release.

Still not run: building `services/Dockerfile.ai` and starting the `ai-service` container (it depends on auth-service, so it needs the full stack).
